# Strategic Rationale (SR) Model

## Overview
This model represents the internal goals, tasks,
qualities and responsibilities of the stakeholders
involved in Use Case 2: Customer Multi-Vendor
Order, Checkout and Payment.

## Actors
- Customer
- Marketplace System
- Producer
- Simulated Payment Service

## Purpose
To explain how each actor achieves its goals
and how its responsibilities contribute to
the multi-vendor ordering process.
