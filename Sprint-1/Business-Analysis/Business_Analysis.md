# Sprint 1 – Business Analysis

## 1. Major Business Processes

Our project focuses on **Use Case 2: Customer Multi-Vendor Order, Checkout and Payment**.

The main processes include customer checkout, stock validation, delivery and collection arrangements, multi-vendor order creation, commission calculations, simulated payments and order confirmation.

## 2. Organisational Responsibilities

- **Customer:** Selects products, checks out and makes payments.
- **Producer:** Supplies products and receives order notifications.
- **Marketplace System:** Manages orders, inventory, commission calculations and confirmations.
- **External Payment Service:** Simulates payment processing and returns payment results.

## 3. Use Case Scope

**In scope:** Customer checkout, stock checks, delivery/collection arrangements, producer-specific orders, the 5% commission, simulated payments, inventory updates, notifications and error handling.

**Out of scope:** Product listing and certification management (Use Case 1), food-quality incident management (Use Case 3), live payment processing and physical delivery operations.

Our objective is to develop a reliable multi-vendor ordering process that connects customers, producers and a simulated payment service.
